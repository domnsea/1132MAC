CHURCH GUEST ZOOM — BUILD 2026-08-23-J

This zip is ChurchGuestZoom.app (not a .command file).

HOW TO RUN

1. Quit 1132wtf-v94 first.
2. Unzip. Drag ChurchGuestZoom.app to the Desktop.
3. Right-click ChurchGuestZoom.app → Open → Open.
4. A window MUST appear immediately: "Church Guest Zoom is starting."
   If you get no window, this is not the J zip.
5. Click Continue. Enter your Mac password if asked.
6. If Keychain asks, click Allow (that saves your gamer login for later).
   If it does not, or you click Deny, guest Zoom still starts and the
   gamer login is left in Keychain so it is not deleted.
7. Leave the app in the Dock until you quit Zoom.

Log: Desktop/ChurchGuestZoom-log.txt
